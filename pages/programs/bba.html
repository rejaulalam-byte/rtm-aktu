Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

The Department of Business Administration offers a four-year Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), which consisted of eight semesters and of total 132 credits. The aim of the BBA program is to prepare students for developing an understanding of the national and global business environment. The curriculum of the program emphasizes on the development of students’ fundamental learning skills as well as communication and computing skills, which are essential to achieve a successful career goal. Specialized and interdisciplinary courses of the program focus on building leadership abilities to create students as future leaders to operate effective in growing technologically advanced national and global marketplace.

Our BBA program concentrate on the following areas:

  • Accounting and Information Systems (AIS),
  • Finance (FIN),
  • Marketing (MKT),
  • Human Resource Management (HRM), and
  • Management Information Systems (MIS).

A student can choose a single major or a double major from the above areas.
Join the programs to acquire the management supremacy of strategic leadership, to enhance your future employability through professional development.

Program Structure:

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is a four-year degree program with an Outcome Based Education (OBE) curriculum. Our program consists of eight semester and of total 132 credits. We offer 2 semesters a year, and each semester comprises 6 months.

Admission Requirements

New students are admitted to the program two times a year – in Spring Semester and Summer Semester. To apply for admission, the candidates will require 12-years of formal education with the expected results as mentioned below:

  • Minimum 2.5 GPA both in SSC or equivalent exam (Dakhil, Vocational) and HSC or Equivalent exams (Alim, Vocational, BM, Diploma in Commerce, Diploma in Business Studies from any group. GPA 2 may be considered provided total GPA in both levels stands at 6.00. Or,
  • ‘O’ level examination 5 subjects and for ‘A’ level examination 2 subjects must be passed respectively from which at least ‘B’ grade or GPA ‘4’ must be obtained in 4 courses and ‘C’ grade or GPA ‘3.5’ must be obtained in the remaining three subjects out of total 7 courses in both the levels. Or,
  • Diploma from a recognized institution with a GPA of 2.00 (out of 4) or equivalent,
  • Completion of 5 (five) O-level subjects and at least 2 (two) A-level subjects. Out of these 7 subjects, applicants must have minimum 4 ‘B’ grade and 3 ‘C’ grades.
  • Sons/daughters of Freedom Fighters with a total GPA of 5.00 in SSC and HSC.
