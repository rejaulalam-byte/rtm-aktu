Executive Master of Business Administration (EMBA)

The Department of Business Administration is offering a world class EMBA degree in the field of business administration which is internationally recognized as the most appreciated business qualification for the students willing to serve in different organizations at the executive, managerial, and administrative positions. Our Program will equip the EMBA students with the knowledge and practical experiences achieved through project works so that they become successful in their professional career. The goal of the program is to produce good quality and efficient graduates who will be able to compete in the job markets and secure top positions in the business enterprises and other fields of management. RTM-AKTU is committed to impart rigorous teaching, fieldworks, industry attachment, reports, presentation and teams projects, all of which will give graduates the necessary abilities to handle real-life business and management challenges or situations. This program will help them to be distinctively apart from others and scan become leaders in their chosen fields.

Our EMBA program concentrates on the following specialized/major areas:

  • Management Science and Operations,
  • Accounting and Information Systems,
  • Finance and Banking,
  • Human Resource Management,
  • Marketing, and
  • Management Information Systems.

Join the programs to acquire the management supremacy of strategic leadership, to enhance your future employability through professional development.

Program Structure:

  • Duration: 1 ½ year. This consists of 3 semesters, and each semester comprises 6 months.
  • Credit requirement: To acquire EMBA degree, a student has to earn 54 credits.
  • Degree requirements: Minimum CGPA 2.50
  • Evaluation system: Outcome-based evaluation system recommended by UGC of Bangladesh.

The 1 ½ -Year EMBA program consists of the following courses:

Course Categories Number of courses Credit per course Total Credit
Foundation Courses 5 3 15
Course Courses 6 3 18
Specialized Elective Courses 4 3 12
Comprehensive Viva-Voce 1 3 03
Project Work with Defense 1 6 06
Total number of credits: 54

Admission Requirements

New students are admitted to the program two times a year – in Spring Semester and Summer Semester. To be eligible to apply for admission in EMBA programs a candidate will require:

  • A Bachelor degree in any discipline from any recognized educational institution with minimum 15 years of schooling.
  • For two-year BA/B.Sc./B.Com. degrees, a Master degree will be required.
  • Two years job experience at the executive level(s) of a recognized, reputed, and well-functioning business or non-business organization is required..

Fee Structure

  • Total Fees: (3 Semesters, 54 credits): After 40% waiver Tk. 79,200
  • Just pay Tk. 15,000 for admission/registration. The rest of the amount can be deposited in the beginning of each semester.

Please visit “Fees & Payments” for details.

Important Notes

  • Application Form price Taka 500.
  • Library caution money Tk. 1,000, which is refundable and thus not included in the total amount.
  • While taking admission, a student has to pay Tk. 15,000 as admission/registration fee.
  • Deposit at least Tk. 15,000 as registration fee during admission and the rest of the amount can be deposited in the beginning of each semester.
  • The semester fee, skill/LAB fee and tuition fee of 1st semester should be deposited within 15 days of the submission of Admission Application Form..

Fee Waiver

  • All students will get a 40% waiver of Semester and Tuition Fees (for 50th Year of Bangladesh’s Independence).
  • The eligible students may get other fee waivers as per RTM-AKTU policy. For more details, please contact admission office.
