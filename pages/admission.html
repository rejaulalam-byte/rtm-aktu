Fee Waiver

  • All students admitted in 2021 (50th Year of Bangladesh’s Independence) will get a 40% waiver on Semester and Tuition Fees.
  • A student with total GPA 10 in SSC & HSC will get additional 20% or total score 9 will get additional 10% fee waiver.
  • Selected poor and meritorious students (3%) will get 100% Waiver on Semester and Tuition Fees (excluding registration fee & skill/practice fees).
  • Selected poor and meritorious dependents (son/daughter) of Freedom Fighters (3%) will get 100% Waiver on Semester and Tuition Fees (excluding registration fee & skill/practice fees).
  • Two or more brother/sister studying at the university will get special 10% Tuition Waiver.
  • A student may get credit waiver based on their previous work experience / academic background, and accordingly fees will be adjusted.
  • A separate application should be submitted to Registrar of the University for such waiver. Based on the university decision, student tuition fees will be adjusted / revised / reduced.
  • In order to encourage students to pay the full annual amount of fees upfront, they ought to be given additional 5% waiver in tuition fees.
  • Continuations of all waivers mentioned above are contingent upon satisfactory performance of the previous year.

Admission Requirements:

For Undergraduate Programs

  • Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC and HSC or equivalent exams.
  • Or, At least one GPA of 2.00 but aggregate GPA of 6.00 in SSC and HSC.
  • Or, ‘O’ level in at least 5 subjects with a minimum GPA 2.50 and ‘A’ level in at least 2 subjects with a minimum GPA 2.00 (A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1).
  • Or, Diploma from a recognized institution with a GPA of 2.0 or equivalent.
  • Or, International Baccalaureate/American High School Diploma.
  • Or, Sons/daughters of Freedom Fighters with an aggregate GPA of 5.00 in SSC and HSC.
  • At least GPA of 2.0 in both SSC and HSC, or equivalent for B.Tech in Fashion Design.

For Postgraduate Programs

  • Students having graduation from a recognized educational institution are eligible to apply.
  • Work experience after graduation in a related field is preferred but not essential.
