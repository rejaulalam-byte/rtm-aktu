About The University

Get to Know RTM Al-Kabir Technical University

RTM Al-Kabir Technical University was established in 2020 in Sylhet with a mission to deliver quality, technology-enabled learning across engineering, business, public health, and education. Our curriculum blends rigorous theory with hands-on studio and lab work, so graduates leave prepared to solve real industry problems from day one.

Guided by experienced faculty and a growing network of research and internship partners, RTM-AKTU is building a campus culture centred on curiosity, integrity, and career readiness — helping every student turn their potential into a profession.

Building Futures

Why a Technical University?

RTM Al-Kabir Technical University’s programs will have built-in elements of flexibility to allow students to personalize their studies and they can develop the knowledge and skills needed for success in the professional world.

  • The University Programs will incorporate practical learning and internship activities. Teaching will be informed by the latest research and industrial trends. The University will foster interdisciplinary connection for hands-on learning opportunities along with the academic curriculum.
  • The University emphasizes the use of state-of-art digital technology and all students will be required to take a compulsory course on Information, Communication and Technology (ICT).
  • The University will recognize the community level and workplace learning for converting them into formally certified diploma/certificates by importing additional skills in selected areas.
  • The University will prepare students to take up the future challenges, and to contribute to the development of society.

Our Values

Code of Conduct

RTM Al-Kabir Technical University (RTM-AKTU) is a Bangladesh-based university committed to prepare students with a broad range of skills through a systematic approach for success in academic study, employment and professional life. RTM-AKTU intends to be a skill based, market driven and industry-focused 21st century global university. This will be accomplished through the use of a comprehensive academic curriculum in combination with workplace internships and hands-on training opportunities.

RTM-AKTU firmly acknowledges that all forms of discrimination and harassment are a violation of human rights and is fully committed that these rights of these individuals are protected in various professional issues including without and regard to gender of all the businesses of the University.

RTM-AKTU’s Code of Conduct is applied by its Board of Trustees (BOT) in order to enhance the workplace environment, the Code of Conduct should be strictly maintained in performing all the businesses of the University.

Core Values Relating to Policy

  • RTM-AKTU believes that sexual and reproductive rights are nationally and internationally recognized human rights and as such should be guaranteed for all citizens.
  • RTM-AKTU is committed to ensure gender equity and to eliminating the discrimination which threatens individual’s wellbeing and leads to the widespread violation of human rights.
