Institutes & Centers Courses

Image Coming Soon

In addition to the Faculties and Departments, RTM-AKTU will establish the following Institute from the start of the University. The Institute will operate under the overall management of the Vice Chancellor of the University and each Institute will be led by Director. It is expected that the institute will be a self-supporting entity and will target poor and under served people. The institute will establish linkages with the national and international organizations to provide support to unrecognized semi-skilled workers, so that they become eligible for in-country and overseas employment. In additions, skill levels will be upgraded to work as small entrepreneurs.

The first Institute will be named as Bangladesh Institute of Inclusive Skill Development (BISD). Key programs of the Institute will include :

  • Post-graduate in Library Science / Computer Science (One year Duration).
  • Diploma / Certificate courses on e.g. ‘Foreign Language’ (English, Arabic, Japanese, and Chinese), ‘Health Care Giver’, Hotel / Tourism Management, Agriculture / Food Technology, Computer Science (Less than one year duration).
  • Diploma Courses in ‘Diploma in Inclusive Skills Development (DISD)’ for professionally unrecognized semi-skilled workers / professionals e.g. Refrigeration and Air-conditioning, Construction Engineering, (Up to 10 months duration).
  • Short Certificate Courses for unrecognized semi-skilled workers in ‘Certificate in Skills Development (CSD)’ e.g. Automobile, Welding, Carpentry, Driving, Tailoring, Electrification, Plumber (3 to 6 months duration).

RTM Al-Kabir Technical University extends its learning and professional development opportunities through its Institutes & Centers Courses, designed to support skill enhancement, practical learning, research, training, and professional capacity building. Under this initiative, the Bangladesh Institute of Skill Development (BISD) focuses on developing practical and industry-relevant skills, while the Center of Research, Training and Management (CRTM) provides opportunities for research, training, and management-focused learning. Together, these initiatives complement the university’s academic programs and create additional pathways for continuous learning and professional growth.
