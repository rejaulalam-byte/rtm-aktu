The Faculty of Science and Engineering at RTM Al-Kabir Technical University is committed to providing quality education in science, technology, and engineering while preparing students to meet the demands of a rapidly evolving technological world.

The Faculty currently offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE). Through a combination of theoretical knowledge, practical learning, laboratory-based education, project work, and problem-solving activities, the Faculty aims to develop technically competent, innovative, and responsible graduates.

With a strong emphasis on academic excellence, practical skills, innovation, and multidisciplinary learning, the Faculty encourages students to explore emerging technologies and develop solutions to real-world challenges. The academic environment is designed to foster creativity, critical thinking, research interest, professional ethics, and lifelong learning.

Our Commitment

The Faculty of Science and Engineering is dedicated to creating an engaging learning environment where students can transform knowledge into practical solutions. By combining quality teaching, hands-on learning, technological awareness, and professional development, the Faculty strives to produce graduates who are capable of contributing meaningfully to industry, society, and the future of technological advancement.
