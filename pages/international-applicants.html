WELCOME INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

RTM Al-Kabir Technical University welcomes students from around the world to become part of a vibrant and diverse academic community. We value the perspectives, experiences, and ideas that international students bring to our classrooms, research activities, and campus life. Our goal is to provide a supportive and inclusive environment where students can feel at home while pursuing their academic and professional aspirations in Bangladesh.

From visa guidance and arrival assistance to accommodation support and academic advising, the University is committed to helping international students make a smooth transition to university life. Our dedicated support services work closely with applicants and students to address their individual needs and provide guidance throughout their academic journey.

Living in Sylhet

Sylhet offers international students a welcoming, peaceful, and relatively affordable environment for study and everyday life. Known for its natural beauty, cultural diversity, and comfortable urban lifestyle, the city provides convenient access to essential services, local transportation, restaurants, shopping areas, healthcare facilities, and other daily necessities. Compared with many major cities, Sylhet offers a moderate cost of living, allowing students to manage accommodation, food, transportation, and personal expenses according to their individual lifestyle and preferences.

With its friendly community, rich cultural heritage, and student-oriented environment, Sylhet provides an ideal setting for international students to focus on their education while experiencing the culture and hospitality of Bangladesh.

International students are encouraged to contact the University for updated guidance on accommodation, transportation, living arrangements, and other practical matters before or after their arrival.

Postgraduate Programs

RTM-AKTU's postgraduate programs are designed for international working professionals and recent graduates seeking advanced specialisation. Delivered through a mix of seminars, research supervision, and applied projects, these programs deepen expertise across engineering, business, and education disciplines while building research and leadership capacity.

Undergraduate Programs

Our four-year undergraduate degrees combine a strong foundation year with progressively specialised coursework, studio and lab work, and a required internship or capstone project. Small class sizes and mentorship from experienced faculty help international students build both technical competence and professional confidence.

Know More

Institutes & Centers Courses

Alongside degree programs, RTM-AKTU runs short courses and certifications through affiliated institutes and centers, including the Bangladesh Institute of Skill Development (BISD) and the Center of Research Training and Management (CRTM). These offerings give international students and working professionals flexible, industry-aligned skill-building outside the standard degree track.
