The Faculty of Education, Health & Technology at RTM Al-Kabir Technical University is committed to advancing education, health, and technology through quality academic programs, practical learning, and a strong focus on professional development. The Faculty aims to prepare graduates with the knowledge, skills, and values required to contribute effectively to the well-being and development of individuals and society.

Bringing together the complementary fields of education, health, and technology, the Faculty provides an interdisciplinary learning environment that encourages students to understand contemporary challenges from multiple perspectives. Through a combination of theoretical knowledge, practical experience, research, and technology-supported learning, students are encouraged to develop critical thinking, creativity, professional competence, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

With an emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, social responsibility, and professional ethics, the Faculty seeks to equip students with the competencies necessary to respond to the evolving needs of the education and health sectors while embracing the opportunities created by technological advancement.

Learning, Innovation and Professional Development

The Faculty places strong emphasis on developing graduates who are not only academically competent but also capable of applying their knowledge in professional and community settings. Students are encouraged to strengthen their communication, analytical, problem-solving, research, and technology-related skills.

By integrating modern educational approaches, health awareness, technological innovation, and practical learning, the Faculty seeks to create an engaging academic environment where students can explore new ideas, develop professional confidence, and prepare for meaningful careers.

Our Commitment

The Faculty of Education, Health & Technology is dedicated to nurturing competent, compassionate, innovative, and socially responsible graduates who can contribute to the advancement of education, healthcare, technology, and society.

Through quality education, practical learning, research, innovation, and professional development, the Faculty strives to prepare graduates to meet the challenges of a rapidly changing world and make a positive and sustainable impact on their communities and beyond.
